Project brief / Integrated marketing / Budget leadership

Build a strategy that scales.

Executing a $1.1M omnichannel marketing program for the South Carolina Army National Guard and authoring a strategy adopted as the model for all 54 states and territories.

OrganizationSouth Carolina Army National Guard
RoleMarketing Director
Role / service contextWithin Guard service, November 2008 – December 2021

54

The focus

Translate statewide recruiting and retention priorities into a coordinated marketing program that could support a distributed recruiting organization.

The program brought digital, social, programmatic, print, and event channels together with a $1.1M marketing budget.

My contribution

  • Authored the state’s annual marketing strategy and executed an omnichannel marketing budget.
  • Supervised a multifunctional marketing team and coordinated with agencies, vendors, and 116 recruiters across 50+ locations.
  • Directed statewide implementation of the National Guard brand refresh, aligning local execution with national standards.
  • Modernized outreach with SEO and programmatic advertising to improve lead quality and conversion tracking.
  • Maintained relationships with the South Carolina Broadcasters Association and local media outlets.

The result

The state achieved recruiting and retention goals for four consecutive years, including during COVID.

The annual marketing strategy I authored was adopted as the model for all 54 states and territories, extending its value beyond South Carolina.

The leadership takeaway

This work required clear priorities, consistent standards, resource allocation, and coordination across a geographically distributed organization.

Build the plan so that local teams can execute it consistently and leaders can understand how the resources support the mission.
