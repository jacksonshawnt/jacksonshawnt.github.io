Project brief / Product marketing / Go-to-market

Turn product value into member adoption.

Partnering with product and engineering teams to bring SaaS-based member engagement tools to market, with 35% adoption among eligible users during go-to-market campaigns.

OrganizationBlueCross BlueShield of South Carolina
RoleMarketing Communications Manager
Role / service contextSeptember 2021 – February 2024

Selected outcomes

35%

Adoption among eligible users during go-to-market campaigns

The focus

Help members understand and use digital engagement tools by connecting product capabilities to clear customer value.

My work spanned SaaS-based member engagement tools and cross-platform campaigns across mobile, web, and app experiences.

My contribution

  • Partnered with product and engineering teams to design and promote member engagement tools.
  • Led cross-platform digital campaign development, aligning messaging across member experiences.
  • Directed internal change management campaigns to align product, sales, and marketing teams during key launches.
  • Used audience engagement analysis to refine positioning and UX messaging for targeted audiences.

The result

Go-to-market campaigns achieved 35% adoption among eligible users for the member engagement tools.

The result reflects a cross-functional effort. My contribution centered on the marketing strategy, product messaging, campaign development, and alignment needed to connect the tools to their intended audience.

The leadership takeaway

This work brought product, engineering, sales, and marketing together around a shared member experience.

Make the product value understandable, then align the experience around the action you want the customer to take.
