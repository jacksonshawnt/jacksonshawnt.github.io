Make the message work harder.

Revamping broker and consultant email campaigns through audience segmentation and optimized messaging, improving open rates by 20% while increasing email volume by 34%.

RoleMarketing Communications Manager
Role / service contextSeptember 2021 – February 2024

Selected outcomes

20%

Improvement in email open rates

The focus

Strengthen broker and consultant communications through more deliberate audience segmentation and messaging.

The email work sat within a broader marketing communications role covering sales enablement, product messaging, and marketing operations.

My contribution

  • Revamped broker and consultant email campaigns.
  • Used segmentation and optimized messaging to improve email engagement.

In the same role, I also spearheaded Dynamics 365 Marketing implementation to improve CRM integration, campaign automation, and reporting accuracy. That broader operational experience complements the email work; it is a separate accomplishment.

The result

The campaign improvements delivered a 20% improvement in open rates, alongside a 34% increase in email volume.

The program combined stronger email engagement with increased communications output.

The leadership takeaway

The work connected audience understanding with the operational discipline needed to sustain a communications program.

A larger communications program needs a clearer understanding of who each message is for and what value it brings.
