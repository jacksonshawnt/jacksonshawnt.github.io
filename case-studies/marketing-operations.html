Case study / Marketing operations

Give the team more room to do the work.

I built a shared calendar and searchable archive so the team could spend less time finding the work and more time moving it forward.

Good work should be easy to find.

Our editorial calendar lived in Word documents shared over email. Older creative and copy lived in individual desktop folders. Finding a previous asset meant asking people to search their files, waiting several minutes, and sometimes still missing the correct version.

The team needed a shared place to plan upcoming work and find what we had already created. Those were connected problems: we were losing time both organizing the next campaign and retrieving material from the last one.

The choice: build around how the team worked.

In 2024, I custom-built a Notion dashboard that brought the editorial calendar and creative and copy archive into one shared workspace. I chose Notion because it supported real-time collaboration, custom dashboards, calendars, and databases at a cost that worked for the team.

The value was having the functions we needed in a place people could use together. We could coordinate current work and search our history without rebuilding the process around another series of emailed documents.

What I owned

As Social Media Manager, I lead a four-person team spanning strategy, creative, and media buying. I designed and built the workspace to support that team’s planning and collaboration.

The dashboard gave the editorial calendar a shared home and made past creative and copy accessible through a searchable archive.

Measure the time the process gives back.

We compared the time individual steps took before and after the change. That process timing supported a measured 30% improvement in team efficiency.

Asset retrieval made the change easy to see: searches that had taken several people minutes across separate folders could be completed in seconds in the shared archive. That example illustrates the workflow improvement; the overall 30% figure came from timing the process steps.
