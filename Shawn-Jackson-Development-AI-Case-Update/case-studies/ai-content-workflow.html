Case study / AI editorial workflow

Make useful answers easier to find.

I built a recurring AI-assisted editorial workflow that connects Ubersuggest research with topic evaluation, content briefs, and article drafts. It saved hours of research per post while keeping accuracy, brand style, and publication with my team.

Build on what the audience was already telling us.

Our blog topics drew on stakeholder feedback, Voice of the Customer insights, current events, and emerging industry themes. Those inputs helped us understand what mattered to the organization and its audiences.

I wanted to add search evidence to that editorial process while reducing the research effort required for each post. The objective was to make useful content easier to discover through search engines and AI-generated answers.

The decision: require relevant research and human review.

I required topic recommendations to be grounded in relevant keyword research and considered alongside existing blog coverage. Ubersuggest provided search and competitive signals that helped evaluate which ideas deserved development and where an article could contribute something useful.

I also made human review a required part of the workflow. My team adjusts the article, checks topic accuracy, and ensures it follows our brand’s style. The team retains responsibility for deciding what is ready to publish.

Give the team a stronger starting point.

I connected research, topic evaluation, briefing, and drafting in an automated recurring workflow. Each run prepares an editorial package with a recommendation, the reasoning behind it, a content brief, and an article draft.

The process reviews existing coverage and editorial standards before developing content. That gives the team a documented basis for the recommendation and a draft they can assess and shape.

Put research time back into editorial judgment.

The workflow saved hours of research per blog post. Bringing research and drafting into one recurring process gave the team more room to refine the article, assess its accuracy, and apply the brand’s style.

After review and approval, publication remains manual. Stronger visibility in search engines and AI-generated answers is the objective guiding the workflow; the observed benefit is research time saved.
