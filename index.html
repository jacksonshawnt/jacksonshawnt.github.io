03 / Leadership

A strategist's lens.
An operator's mindset.

My career spans health care marketing, digital product engagement, and military marketing leadership. The common thread: helping people understand the value, then building the team and plan to deliver it.

At BlueCross BlueShield of South Carolina, I've led work across product adoption, enterprise social strategy, paid media, and marketing operations. Before that, I brought statewide recruiting priorities to life through an integrated National Guard marketing program.

I bring a Bachelor of Arts in Digital Marketing, experience managing substantial budgets, and a leadership approach grounded in clear expectations, thoughtful collaboration, and measurable progress.
