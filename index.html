How I lead

Purpose gives
the work direction.

My work has taken me from statewide National Guard marketing to healthcare communications, digital product launches, and enterprise social strategy. Across those settings, I’ve learned that people need to understand why the work matters before a plan can earn their commitment.

That is how I approach leadership. I set direction, help people develop, and connect the team’s daily decisions to the larger objective. Clear expectations make it easier for people to contribute and understand the value of their work.

Today, I lead social and paid media strategy across four brands at BlueCross BlueShield of South Carolina and own a $250K+ annual paid media budget. I develop a multidisciplinary team and guide campaign decisions, operating improvements, and communications when issues become sensitive.
